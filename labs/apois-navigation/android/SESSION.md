# Lifecycle contract — laboratory
NavigationSessionController models the lifetime of native navigation independently of ride business rules. Disabled initially. Pickup -> waiting PIN stops the pickup navigator; server-confirmed trip starts a fresh generation. Cancellation/completion closes the leg and releases voice; old route responses are rejected by token. Back/finish/rating/fallback are requests, not database changes. Background suppresses speech until foreground (conservative lab policy, not yet a production driving decision); the existing APOIS GPS service remains responsible for tracking. Host must serialize effects, implement authoritative snapshots, revalidate IDs/status with the server, close native resources, acknowledge every action with a newer revision including failures, and wire actual callbacks to Ferrostar. These are unit-tested contracts, not a wired Capacitor bridge. No changes to production.
